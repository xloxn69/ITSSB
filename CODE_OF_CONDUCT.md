# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback
gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users, many of
whom are students and families.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, and publishing anyone's private information (including students'
names, addresses, or locations) without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](../../security/advisories/new) or by
contacting a maintainer listed in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS).
All reports are reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove
contributions or block contributors who do not follow this code.
